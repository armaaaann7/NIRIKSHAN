CORE CAPABILITIES

Designed for repeatable, explainable monitoring

01

Consistent capture

Each monitored area has a simple name such as “Balcony · 3rd floor”. The first accepted photo becomes its starting reference automatically.

02

Image alignment

The system attempts to register the new image against a trusted reference before comparing visible changes.

03

Visible-change evidence

The score accumulates progression, multiple crack-like regions and structural context instead of escalating a building because of one isolated mark.

04

Human-in-the-loop

Engineers confirm findings, mark false alarms or request a site visit. NIRIKSHAN never replaces professional judgement.

METHOD, LIMITS & RESPONSIBLE USE

What the prototype does — and what it deliberately does not claim

The current build is an engineering-assist prototype. Its purpose is to organise visible evidence, not replace a structural inspection.

01

WHAT IT DOES

Longitudinal visible-change monitoring

NIRIKSHAN compares repeated exterior smartphone images of the same simple building area, such as a balcony, wall or column, and highlights visible changes that may deserve engineer review.

02

CURRENT PIPELINE

Registration before comparison

The prototype first checks whether two images are comparable, performs image registration and illumination normalisation, then evaluates crack-candidate change.

03

LIMITATION

No structural safety verdict

The Inspection Priority Score is a transparent review-order heuristic. It is not collapse probability, structural certification, or proof that a building is safe or unsafe.

04

AI ROADMAP

Model-ready vision layer

The segmentation layer is isolated so the current classical baseline can later be replaced with a trained U-Net, DeepLabV3+, SegFormer or YOLO-seg model.

05

PRIVACY

Exterior evidence only

Use legally captured exterior imagery only. Production deployment requires role-based access, privacy filtering, hardened storage and audit logging.

06

HUMAN REVIEW

Engineer remains the decision-maker

Every result is designed to be reviewed with its evidence. Uncertain captures are quarantined rather than silently influencing the queue.

07

PRIORITY LOGIC

Evidence accumulates before priority rises

One isolated crack-like region normally stays in routine monitoring. Multiple new regions, repeated progression and structurally relevant elements raise inspection priority.

08

STRUCTURAL STRENGTH

Never guessed from a photo

Structure type and verified engineering records provide context. If strength or test data is unavailable, the product explicitly shows “Not assessed”.

Important: NIRIKSHAN is a prototype for visual monitoring and inspection prioritisation. It must not be used as a substitute for a structural engineer, statutory inspection or emergency response.
